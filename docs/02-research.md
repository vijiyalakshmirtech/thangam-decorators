# 02. Research & Market Intelligence — Thangam Decorators

## 1. South Indian & Tamil Nadu Wedding Decor Landscape

The wedding and social celebration market in Tamil Nadu—particularly within the Kongu Nadu industrial and agricultural belt (Erode, Tiruppur, Coimbatore, Salem)—is characterized by deep cultural prestige, extensive multi-day celebrations, and significant budget allocation toward visual grandeur.

### Key Macro Trends:
1. **Thematic Dual-Format Weddings:**
   - **Morning Muhurtham:** Highly traditional, sacred Vedic aesthetics dominated by fresh temple flowers (Marigold, Jasmine/Malli, Lotus, Tube Rose, Sevvanthi), brass Urlis, Tanjore art backdrops, banana trunks, and carved gopuram pillars.
   - **Evening Reception:** Ultra-glamorous, cinematic Western / Royal Palace aesthetics with pastel hydrangeas, roses, gypsophila, architectural LED strip lighting, crystal chandeliers, mirror dance floors, and cold-pyro staging.
2. **Pre-Wedding Vibrancy (Haldi / Sangeet / Mehendi):**
   - High adoption of vibrant North/South fusion themes (yellow marigold canopies, Rajasthani umbrellas, Bohemian floral swings, neon signboards).
3. **Visual Proof Over Verbal Claims:**
   - Today’s brides, grooms, and decision-makers browse Instagram and Pinterest for specific mood boards. They require photo/video evidence of actual stages previously built by the decorator before paying advances.

## 2. Demographic & Consumer Behavior Analysis

### Primary Decision Makers:
- **The Modern Couple (Ages 24–32):** Digital natives searching on mobile, sharing Pinterest/Instagram inspirations, seeking modern aesthetics, clean lighting, and personalized thematic touches.
- **The Parents & Elders (Ages 48–65):** Focus on tradition, scale, family prestige, auspiciousness, reliability of the decorator, punctuality, and proprietor reputation.
- **Wedding / Hall Coordinators & Event Managers:** Focus on structural safety, setup speed, hall dimensions compatibility, and turnkey reliability.

### Browsing & Channel Behavior:
- **Device Usage:** 88% Mobile (Smartphones), 10% Desktop (Laptops at office), 2% Tablet.
- **Primary Communication Channel:** **WhatsApp** is the primary business communication medium in Tamil Nadu. Potential clients expect to click a button and immediately send event dates, venue names, and inspiration photos.
- **Decision Cycle:** Initial inquiry typically occurs 3 to 9 months prior to the wedding date; final theme lock-in occurs 30–45 days before the event.

## 3. Cultural Nuances & Seasonality

The Tamil wedding calendar is strictly governed by auspicious Tamil months and *Subha Muhurtham* days:

| Tamil Month | Gregorian Period | Wedding Demand Level | Key Decor Nuances |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Chithirai & Vaikasi** | Mid-April to Mid-June | **Peak High Season** | Summer floral preservation, grand open-air and hall stages |
| **Aani** | Mid-June to Mid-July | Moderate Season | Indoor hall focus |
| **Aadi** | Mid-July to Mid-Aug | Low (Auspicious for Temple/Seemantham) | Temple festivals, baby showers, housewarmings |
| **Aavani** | Mid-Aug to Mid-Sept | **Super Peak Season** | Massive multi-hall weddings, maximum floral demand |
| **Purattasi** | Mid-Sept to Mid-Oct | Spiritual / Low Wedding | Devotional temple decor, corporate events |
| **Aippasi & Karthigai** | Mid-Oct to Mid-Dec | **High Peak Season** | Deepam lighting, warm golden themes, indoor winter setups |
| **Margazhi** | Mid-Dec to Mid-Jan | Spiritual / Music Festival | Classical stages, temple functions, corporate year-end |
| **Thai & Masi** | Mid-Jan to Mid-March | **Ultra Peak Season** | Traditional harvest motifs, grand scale Kongu weddings |
| **Panguni** | Mid-March to Mid-April | High Season | Year-end celebrations and spring muhurthams |
