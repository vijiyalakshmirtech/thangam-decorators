## 2. Navigation Architecture

### Primary Header Navigation (Desktop & Mobile Drawer):
- **Brand Logo:** `THANGAM DECORATORS` (P.T. Selvam)
- **Navigation Links:**
  - `Home`
  - `About P.T. Selvam & Thangam`
  - `Decor Services` (Dropdown with service categories)
  - `Stage Portfolio` (With direct category anchors)
  - `Our Process`
  - `Testimonials`
  - `Contact Us`
- **Header CTAs:**
  - `Book Free Consultation` (Button -> Quote Form / WhatsApp)
  - `Call Hotline` (Click-to-Call icon / direct phone number)

### Persistent Mobile Sticky Bottom Bar:
- 📞 **Call P.T. Selvam** (Direct phone link)
- 💬 **WhatsApp Inquiry** (Direct pre-filled WhatsApp link)
- 🖼️ **View Stages** (Quick jump to portfolio)
- 📝 **Get Quote** (Instant modal / quote anchor)

### Comprehensive Footer Architecture:
- **Col 1: Brand Authority:** Thangam Decorators logo, P.T. Selvam signature, short mission summary, social links.
- **Col 2: Signature Services:** Links to Mandapam, Reception, Haldi, Temple decor, and 3D stage rendering.
- **Col 3: Key Locations Served:** Erode, Perundurai, Bhavani, Gobichettipalayam, Tiruppur, Salem, Coimbatore, Namakkal, Karur.
- **Col 4: Direct Contact & Office:** Full Erode address, WhatsApp number, phone number, operating hours, GST registration info `[CLIENT INFORMATION REQUIRED]`.
