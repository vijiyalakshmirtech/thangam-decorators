# 06. User Journey & Personas — Thangam Decorators

## 1. Target User Personas

### Persona A: "The Aspirational Bride / Groom" (Priya & Karthik)
- **Profile:** 26–30 years old, software professional or entrepreneur based in Coimbatore/Chennai/Bangalore, holding wedding in Erode/Kongu family mandapam.
- **Goals:** Wants an Instagram-worthy, bespoke wedding reception stage with contemporary floral arches, dramatic lighting, and a distinct theme that looks modern yet culturally elegant.
- **Pain Points:** Hard to find reliable high-fashion stage decorators outside Chennai/Bangalore who can replicate Pinterest boards without exorbitant outstation charges.
- **Desired Action on Site:** Browse high-res stage photos by theme -> Select favorite stage -> Click WhatsApp to send date, venue, and stage photo to P.T. Selvam for availability.

### Persona B: "The Respected Family Elder / Decision Maker" (Ramasamy Gounder)
- **Profile:** 54–62 years old, industrialist/landowner in Erode/Tiruppur, funding daughter's/son's grand wedding.
- **Goals:** Wants a grand, dignified, traditional South Indian Muhurtham Mandapam with authentic temple aesthetics, pure fresh flowers, and absolute assurance of on-time delivery before the auspicious muhurtham hour.
- **Pain Points:** Fears unreliable decorators who show 3D designs but fail to deliver quality flowers, arrive late, or have structural instability on stage.
- **Desired Action on Site:** See proof of P.T. Selvam’s personal reputation, scale of past mandapams, physical office in Erode -> Call the proprietor directly.

### Persona C: "The NRI / Remote Planner" (Senthil & Deepa)
- **Profile:** 32 years old, living in California/Singapore, managing wedding remotely for Erode hometown ceremony.
- **Goals:** Needs clear digital communication, 3D stage mockups, transparent pricing packages, and turnkey assurance.
- **Pain Points:** Time zone differences, lack of local visibility, fear of being overcharged.
- **Desired Action on Site:** Review 3D visualization capabilities, submit detailed quote form with date/mandapam name, receive custom proposal via WhatsApp/Email.

## 3. Friction Points & UX Mitigations

| Friction / User Hesitation | Cause | UX Mitigation on Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"Are these photos real or downloaded from Pinterest?"* | Scepticism of online decor portfolios | Real on-site photos, video walkthroughs, and watermarked tags of Thangam Decorators and P.T. Selvam. |
| *"Will they be available on my specific Muhurtham date?"* | Peak season booking anxiety | Instant WhatsApp date checker CTA on hero and sticky bottom bar. |
| *"Is the pricing within my budget?"* | Ambiguity around luxury decor costs | Clear tiered service descriptions and a non-intimidating "Get Custom Estimate" form. |
| *"Can they handle large 50-foot wide stages?"* | Scale requirement for grand halls | Portfolio filters by stage size and featured mega-mandapam case studies. |
