# 03. Competitor Analysis — Thangam Decorators

## 1. Competitor Landscape Overview

The event decoration ecosystem in Western Tamil Nadu comprises three tiers:
1. **Tier 1 — Elite Statewide Design Houses & Destination Stylists:** Chennai/Bangalore/Coimbatore-based design agencies (e.g., The A-Cube Project, Vivaah, Marriage Colours). They command high budgets, feature slick websites, but have high overhead and travel costs for Erode clients.
2. **Tier 2 — Established Regional Kongu Decorators:** Leading decorators based in Erode, Coimbatore, Salem, and Tiruppur. They possess strong local Mandapam relationships and large inventory, but often have outdated, slow, or template-based websites with disorganized image dumps.
3. **Tier 3 — Hyper-Local Florists & Sound/Light Contractors:** Neighborhood vendors with minimal brand identity, no web presence, operating purely via word of mouth and basic WhatsApp photos.

## 3. Common Competitor Weaknesses & Our Strategic Advantage

### 1. The "Heavy Image Problem"
- **Competitor Flaw:** Competitor sites load 5MB–10MB uncompressed camera JPEGs, causing mobile browsers on 4G in Erode/Salem to stall.
- **Thangam Decorators Solution:** Next-gen WebP/AVIF responsive image formats, progressive BlurHash placeholders, and dynamic CDN asset delivery.

### 2. Lack of Categorization
- **Competitor Flaw:** Muhurtham, Birthday, Haldi, and Reception stages are jumbled together, frustrating couples looking for a specific stage style.
- **Thangam Decorators Solution:** Tag-based filtering system (e.g., *Traditional Temple Mandapam*, *Royal Palace Reception*, *Pastel Floral Arch*, *Outdoor Lawn Stage*).

### 3. Missing Clear Social Proof & Process
- **Competitor Flaw:** No clear explanation of how the decorator works from initial 3D stage concept to final delivery.
- **Thangam Decorators Solution:** Transparent 5-step workflow (Consultation -> 3D Visualization -> Material Selection -> Rigging & Styling -> Flawless Teardown).
