# 01. Business Brief — Thangam Decorators

## 1. Executive Overview

**Thangam Decorators** is an established event scenography, stage design, and wedding decoration business based in **Erode, Tamil Nadu, India**, owned and operated by **P.T. Selvam**. The business delivers stage setups for weddings, receptions, traditional South Indian family ceremonies (Nichayathartham, Muhurtham, Seemantham), cultural festivals, and community celebrations.

The primary digital objective is to establish an authentic, high-performing, professional web presence that effectively showcases Thangam Decorators' real work, reinforces trust in P.T. Selvam’s craftsmanship, and streamlines client inquiries across Erode and Western Tamil Nadu.

## 3. Core Services

1. **Traditional South Indian Wedding Mandapams:**
   - Temple gopuram architectures, carved pillars, fresh flower strings (Malli, Lotus, Marigold), and Muhurtham sacred setups.
2. **Grand Reception Stages:**
   - Contemporary floral backdrops, arch framing, lighting setups, drapery, and seating arrangements.
3. **Pre-Wedding & Family Ceremonies:**
   - Haldi & Mehendi setups, Engagement (Nichayathartham) backdrops, Seemantham (Baby Shower), and Gruhapravesam decor.
4. **Temple & Cultural Events:**
   - Temple festival arches, deity floral decor, and community stage setups.
5. **Stage Lighting & Ambience Support:**
   - Par cans, warm uplighting, truss framing, and ambient backlighting.
