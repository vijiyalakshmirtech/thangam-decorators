# 04. Website Strategy — Thangam Decorators

## 1. Brand Strategic Positioning

**Thangam Decorators** is positioned as:
> **"Erode's Premier Luxury Stage & Wedding Scenography Atelier — Where Sacred Heritage Meets Regal Splendor."**

The website is designed not merely as an informational brochure, but as an **authoritative digital flagship and high-converting consultation concierge** that immediately commands respect, demonstrates artistic mastery, and instills complete confidence in high-net-worth families and wedding couples.

## 3. Conversion Funnel & Lead Acquisition Strategy

### Level 1: Immediate Inspiration & Hook (Above the Fold)
- High-impact cinematic visual reel / photography of signature wedding stages.
- Value proposition badge: *"Handcrafted Staging, Floral Architecture & Luxury Event Design by P.T. Selvam"*.
- Primary CTAs: **"Explore Signature Collections"** and **"Instant WhatsApp Consultation"**.

### Level 2: Interactive Exploration & Segmentation
- Filterable gallery allowing couples to view specific aesthetics:
  - *Traditional South Indian Mandapam*
  - *Grand Royal Reception*
  - *Pastel & Floral Elegance*
  - *Vibrant Haldi / Mehendi*
  - *Corporate & Spiritual Gala*
- Each project card contains an **"Inquire About This Stage"** CTA that pre-fills the inquiry with the exact stage ID / theme name.

### Level 3: Trust & Credibility Building
- The "P.T. Selvam Standard" — Highlighting 100% on-time completion record, fresh floral sourcing, in-house master artisans, and structural safety.
- Testimonials from real couples and families across Erode, Tiruppur, Coimbatore, and Salem.

### Level 4: Seamless Multi-Channel Conversion
- **Floating WhatsApp Widget:** Persistent on mobile with smart quick options (*"Hi P.T. Selvam, I want to check availability for my wedding on [Date]"*).
- **Direct Phone Hotline:** Click-to-call button prominently placed on navigation and mobile sticky footer.
- **Detailed Quote Form:** For clients who prefer providing venue, date, guest count, and expected budget.

---

## 4. Tone of Voice & Copywriting Framework

- **Regal & Elegant:** High vocabulary that honors the sacred and joyful gravity of weddings (*scenography, bespoke, grand mandapam, heirloom elegance*).
- **Warm & Respectful (Kongu Hospitality):** Gracious, accommodating, and rooted in traditional South Indian family values.
- **Authoritative & Reassuring:** Direct assurances on punctuality, pristine floral freshness, robust staging safety, and transparent pricing.
